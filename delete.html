Flexa — Account Deletion

How to delete your Flexa account and associated data.

You may request deletion of your Flexa account and all personal data associated with it at any time. Deletion is processed by emailing our support address.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@flexa.app from the email address you use to sign in to Flexa.
  2. Use the subject line “Account Deletion Request”.
  3. No other information is required — the sign-in email is what we use to identify your account.

What gets deleted

  • Your Flexa account record (email, sign-in timestamps, app version)
  • Your filter preferences and settings
  • Your in-app activity history
  • Your subscription state and trial history (the subscription itself is cancelled separately via Google Play)
  • Support correspondence linked to your account

Timeline

We will action verified deletion requests within 30 days of receipt and email you a confirmation when the deletion is complete.

What we may retain

A minimal record of the deletion request itself (email, request date, deletion date) is kept for fraud-prevention, tax, and legal-compliance purposes. This record contains no in-app activity data and is kept only as long as required by law.

Cancelling your subscription

Cancelling your Google Play subscription is a separate action from account deletion. You can cancel at any time via Google Play → Subscriptions → Flexa, with or without requesting deletion.

Questions

For questions about deletion or your data, contact support@flexa.app. For the full privacy policy, see our Privacy Policy.
